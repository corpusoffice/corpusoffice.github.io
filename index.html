Soonha Hwang

Assistant Professor of German Linguistics Department of German Language and Literature, Hanyang University

I study variation and structural choice in German, with a focus on syntax, word order, and argument structure. My work combines corpus linguistics and quantitative methods with theoretically motivated analysis.
